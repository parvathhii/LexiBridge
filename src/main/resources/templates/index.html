College Major Project

Empowering Neurodivergent Learners with Accessible Technology

LexiBridge is a full-featured assistive educational platform built for children and learners with dyslexia. Featuring tailored typography, auditory feedback, 8 phonemic games, cognitive error tracking, and role-based portals for students, parents, teachers, and admins.
